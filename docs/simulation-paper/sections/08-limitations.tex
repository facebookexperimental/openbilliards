\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

Every limitation in this section is either a row of the machine-readable
deviation ledger kept in the artifact bundle (38 rows across
nine categories; per-category counts in
Table~\ref{tab:waiver-categories}, Section~\ref{sec:methodology}) or a
declared scope boundary of the study itself.
The resolved rows and the oracle waiver are treated in
Section~\ref{sec:oracle}; the declared-model-content rows and the frame
artifact are reported alongside their experiments in
Section~\ref{sec:results}. The remaining categories --- those that bound
what this paper's conclusions may claim --- are collected here, by
category.

\subsection{From the waiver ledger, by category}

\paragraph{Registered-band misses (seven rows)}
Seven registered acceptance bands or source claims were missed.
Following the anti-overfit doctrine of Section~\ref{sec:methodology} ---
and ASME V\&V~20's position that validation is not pass/fail
\citep{asme2009vv20} --- each miss is measured and characterized, no
band was re-tuned, and each row's full characterization lives in the
ledger itself (artifact bundle). (i)~The 30$^\circ$-rule peak height is in-band
only at slow speed ($E = -0.83^\circ$ at 2.0\,m/s; band membership
itself $e_b$-fragile, Section~\ref{sec:results-a}). (ii)~One of 240 anchored
cushion-rebound points from \citet{mathavan2010cushion} misses at the
band edge ($E = +0.0577$ against 0.056), a tolerance-edge flip recorded
as a miss all the same.
(iii)~Under the calibrated Stronge cushion pairing, the 30$^\circ$
sliding-bank speed-independence anchor spreads $+2.71^\circ$ against its
$\leq 1.9^\circ$ window, and the 45$^\circ$ sliding bank
\emph{lengthens} with speed where the source claims shortening; the admissible-space sweep of
Section~\ref{sec:results-c} attributes the \emph{magnitude} of the
speed-direction reversal to
the interaction of the fitted $\mu(v_{\mathrm{slip}})$ curves
with the tangential-compliance pairings --- over the full cross-form
crossing every
flat-$\mu_w$ cell restores the source direction (270 of 270) and
every fitted-curve~$\times$~compliant-pairing cell reverses (54 of
54), while the fitted curves leave the non-compliant
forms source-correct in 44 of 54 cells (the exceptions, Han under
the \code{hsv_fitted} curve, flip by $+0.28$ to $+2.72^\circ$ off the
default operating point, still well below this row's reversals at
matched corners) --- so this row's reversal magnitude is content of
the
calibrated compliant pairing. (iv)~Three further
banking
targets fail as pinned, documented gates --- steep rolling banks go long
under every preset (\code{han2005_default} tangent ratio 1.701 against
the $\leq 1.45$ cap), and draw \emph{lengthens} the Han impact rebound
where the source ordering says shorten; the same sweep and
crossing find
the draw-ordering reversal in \emph{every} Han/Stronge cell --- all
210 crossing cells, both fitted curves included --- and in no cell of
the shipped tangential-compliance Mathavan form (0 of 84; its
compliance-free variant flips in 6 of 84 cells at flat
$\mu_w \geq 0.25$, by at most $+0.52^\circ$), i.e.\ a model-form
boundary
of the single-contact impulse class rather than a parameter choice.
All of these banking
residuals are bit-identical across cushion branch-selection variants ---
none is a remnant of the resolved branch-selection defect.
(v)~The frozen all-six bank criterion is met at only five of six aims
--- five are inside the envelope and the criterion fails on one: aim 1.0
banks 0.108 diamonds beyond the five-table TP~B.28 envelope
\citep{alciatore_tp_b28}.
(vi)~Break effective mass scales with the connected cluster but caps at
$E = -0.14$ below the high-speed-video concept value of 1.5
\citep{alciatore_hsv_catalog}, a scaling the sequential baseline erases
entirely. (vii)~The trisect rule
holds within 10\% of its $3\times$ factor only for $22.5$--$40^\circ$
cuts, a miss of the registered ``below $40^\circ$'' phrasing that we
regard as the study's flagship domains-of-validity result rather than a
defect (Section~\ref{sec:results-f}).

\paragraph{Convention sensitivities (four rows)}
Four results depend on an input or measurement convention; both readings
are recorded per row, one adopted with rationale. The squirt catalog's
tip-center reading of the robot protocol undershoots all four
robot-measured shafts by $0.32$--$0.42^\circ$, with the contact
re-reading closing the gap (Section~\ref{sec:results-b}). The miscue limit is a one-sidedly
conservative commanded-offset envelope with no tip-friction model,
implied tip friction 0.39--0.58. The
jump chart's overhead clearance criterion overstates reach by up to one
grid step at the window's far edge, against the sphere criterion the
paper adopts. And oracle parity is
pinned permanently to the pre-fix slip law, so the oracle channel
measures the slip-law convention gap ($0.1301^\circ$ in throw) by design
(Section~\ref{sec:oracle}).

\paragraph{Post-registration input-envelope amendment}
After the registered baseline was recorded, the kernel's input-envelope
convention was revised (kernel
\code{0.1.0+8efd660e}): the miscue limit now binds the ball-surface
\emph{contact} offset --- the variable in which the $0.5$--$0.55R$
literature bracket is stated --- raising the commanded tip-center cap
from $0.5R$ to $0.5\,(1 + r_{\mathrm{tip}}/R)$, i.e.\ $0.686R$ for the
shipped nickel tip (contact offset exactly $0.5R$ at the cap). The
revision widens input validation only, and bit-exact reproduction
holds for every previously \emph{accepted} input --- which includes
every simulated trajectory in the battery. One behavioral change class
exists and is disclosed rather than absorbed: \expid{B3}'s registered
rejection probes are boundary probes \emph{of} the registered envelope
itself, and the nine run under the nickel tip (eight sweep-tail cells
at commanded $0.525$--$0.6R$, contact $0.383$--$0.438R$, plus one
combined-axis probe) would be accepted and simulate under the revised
convention, while the eight contact-mode cells still reject (their
contact offsets exceed the $0.5R$ contact limit under every
convention). Those recorded rejections are convention-bound results of
the registered kernel, not reproducible behaviors of the revised one;
the pinned \expid{B3} artifact stands unchanged as the
registered-envelope record referenced by the registered results, while
post-registration maintenance re-baselines under the revised envelope
are recorded in the study's baseline manifest. No simulated
registered result changes, and the registered battery results remain
pinned to the \code{0.1.0+fb3425ad} lineage
(Section~\ref{sec:reproducibility}). The contact-offset band
$(0.5, 0.55]R$ remains unreachable --- the envelope stays on the
conservative side of the literature bracket.

\paragraph{Source discrepancies (three rows)}
Three deviations belong to the source material itself; the battery
records both readings. The 2005 printing of one technical proof fits
the ball--ball friction decay with $c = 0.77$ where the current
revision --- and the kernel --- use $c = 1.088$; each document
transcription-checks against its own form
\citep{alciatore_tp_catalog,alciatore_tp_a14}. Seven points in
\citet{mathavan2010cushion} are internally inconsistent between the
paper's equations and its printed figures; the kernel reproduces the
equations, and the points are excluded with that attribution. The corner-five fourth-rail lore
splits against itself: the ``one diamond short'' benchmark plays
on-system ($n_4 = -0.02$ diamonds) while the secondary speed and
english direction claims measure sign-reversed.

\paragraph{Preset validity domains (three rows)}
The fitted equipment presets carry documented validity domains rather
than blanket endorsements. \code{grip_fitted} is out of domain on
bank/kick observables in three independent measurement frames (worst: a
$+9.6^\circ$ bank speed spread against a $\leq 1.9^\circ$ window) and
has been demoted from new selection in the application layer, the model
retained and persisted selections grandfathered. \code{mathavan_fitted} is in-domain for
kick/rebound spin coupling and the snooker pairing, out-of-domain for
shallow-aim US-pool banks and for the lag (3.45 table lengths against
the WPA 4.0--4.5 window; Section~\ref{sec:results-f}); per the
anti-overfit doctrine it stays opt-in and is not re-tuned. And the fitted tangential-compliance
presets break kick mirror symmetry at up to $\sim 1.1\times10^{-2}$
diamonds through last-ulp branch divergence --- verification-side
numerical noise, recorded per cell.

\paragraph{Open investigations (three rows)}
One physics investigation remains open: the compliant resolver's
frozen-pair combination throw exceeds the measured $\sim 1.04\times$
frozen excess at $1.13$--$1.55\times$, speed-dependent, while the
sequential baseline shows no excess at all --- the sign is right, the
magnitude is not, the row stays open as the honest
edge of contribution C3, and its possible contamination of the
\expid{E1} conclusion is bounded, not assumed away
(Section~\ref{sec:results-e}). The
other two rows are bookkeeping on internal worksheet and note strings
whose residuals equal the slip-law shift by design and touch no measured
column.

\subsection{Scope limitations outside the ledger}

\paragraph{Determinism audit gaps}
The determinism audit matrix of Section~\ref{sec:reproducibility} has
gaps, which we state as untested rather than argued away. No trajectory
hashes have been collected on SpiderMonkey, JavaScriptCore, or any
non-browser WebAssembly runtime such as wasmtime; there is no Windows leg
and no linux-arm64 or macOS-x86\_64 leg. (The battery-through-wasm cell
is closed by measurement: matrix row 11,
Section~\ref{sec:reproducibility}.) The bundle wheel's version stamp
is coherent with the expected-hash manifest only up to a declared
normalization until the planned submission-freeze rebuild, and no
non-author has yet executed the tier-1 protocol. The structural argument
for the untested engines --- kernel-internal transcendentals compiled
into the binary, a NaN-free state invariant, no relaxed SIMD
\citep{wasm2015nondeterminism} --- is given in
Section~\ref{sec:reproducibility}; the posture remains \emph{measure,
don't argue}.

\paragraph{Model scope}
The physical model is bounded by design. There is no air drag and no
Magnus force: the deliberate constraint that keeps ball motion piecewise
polynomial and event detection closed-form. Cloth wear and humidity are
not modeled --- precisely the unspecified ``typical pool table''
condition class that the fourth-rail lore rows implicate. Cushion models inherit their sources'
validity domains: the rigid-cushion analysis of
\citet{mathavan2010cushion} is declared valid to 2.5\,m/s by its authors,
and beyond that domain the kernel overlay overshoots monotonically, from
$+0.018$\,m/s at 2.53\,m/s to $+0.359$\,m/s at 3.40\,m/s. Vertical-channel (hop) physics after the
break is constrained only by a one-sided bound: the kernel's post-break
cue-ball hop apex is $\leq 0.039$ ball diameters everywhere, satisfying
the footage's one-sided $\lesssim 0.3$-diameter expectation, but the
observed clip value of $\sim 0.23$ diameters shows the vertical channel
is under-constrained by the available evidence.

\paragraph{Anchor-material caveats}
Three caveats travel with the validation data. The first-party
table-session footage carries a leg-rotation orientation drift (an open
item). The high-speed-video footage used for cushion anchors was
not recorded on a K-66 rail profile, so cushion-geometry conclusions
drawn from it do not transfer unmodified to regulation US-pool rails
\citep{alciatore_hsv_catalog}. And the lag residual on the fitted
Mathavan pairing is stated with its preset-domain row above.

\paragraph{Source availability and the kernel revision of record}
The kernel source is public under the MIT license, with the portions
derived from pooltool under the Apache License~2.0, so readers can
inspect the implementation and rebuild it. The public history, however,
begins at a snapshot later than this paper's canonical kernel
\code{0.1.0+fb3425ad}, and the canonical binary of record, the experiment
scripts, pinned outputs, expected-hash manifest, and ledger belong to an
artifact bundle that has not been published
(Section~\ref{sec:availability}). This paper's
figures and hashes therefore cannot be regenerated from the public tree,
and until the bundle is published the study's independently checkable
surface is the public source, the reported outputs, and the kernel's own
anchor fixtures rather than a re-run of the battery.
Section~\ref{sec:reproducibility} describes the mitigations that take
effect once the bundle is published --- a hash-defined artifact of
record, one regeneration script per figure, and planned third-party
attestation.

\paragraph{Chaotic outcome bits at break energy}
Contribution C4 is a per-binary claim. Bit-exactness holds for a fixed
binary across platforms, runtimes, and runs; it does not extend across
model revisions, where downstream outcome bits of long slip cascades at
break-scale energies can flip. At the post-cascade re-baseline, three
extreme break rows (quarter-ball offsets at 12--13.4\,m/s) flipped a
scratch outcome bit, with no registered band attached. The paper claims
bit-exact reproduction of every figure from the canonical binary; it does
not claim insensitivity of chaotic outcome bits to model evolution.
