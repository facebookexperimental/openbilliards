\section*{Acknowledgments}
\label{sec:acknowledgments}

This work draws throughout on David G.~Alciatore's technical-proof,
high-speed-video, and FAQ corpus
\citep{alciatore2008coriolis,alciatore_tp_catalog,%
alciatore_hsv_catalog,alciatore_faq}, used under the posted reuse policy
(verified 2026-07-21): free use with clear citation and links. No
technical-proof figure or high-speed-video frame is reproduced in this
paper or redistributed with it; the validation data derived

Explicit written permission will be sought before any camera-ready
reproduction of source figures, and any permitted figures will be
enumerated here.

We thank the pooltool project \citep{kiefl2024pooltool}, which serves as

are documented.
